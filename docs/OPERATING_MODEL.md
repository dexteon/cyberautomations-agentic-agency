# Operating Model

## Core concept

CyberAutomations should run as a master business crew with several specialist teams under it:

- Leadership team
- Growth and prospect intelligence team
- Competitive intelligence team
- Prototype and solutions team
- Content and distribution team
- Intake and onboarding team
- Support and QA team
- Security review layer

## Suggested execution lanes

1. Growth lane: prospect discovery, pain extraction, scoring, outreach package.
2. Content lane: blog topics, authority assets, social adaptations, Reddit intelligence.
3. Offer lane: prototype plan, diagnostics, scope shaping.
4. Intake lane: lead capture, qualification, proposal, onboarding.
5. Governance lane: QA review, human approval, structured logs.

## Human review rule

Every forward-facing post, outreach draft, teardown, blog article, or proposal should stop at a review gate before release.

## Multi-tenant rule

Even while starting with Example Co, store tenant metadata explicitly and avoid hardcoded single-client assumptions in file paths, collection names, or workflow state.
